1. Overview & Single Purpose

MapsPulse is a specialized productivity browser extension for Chrome designed with a single, transparent purpose: to allow users to bookmark, tag, and organize local business place records visited on Maps into a private local CRM workspace and export them into structured spreadsheet formats (CSV, TSV, JSON).

Strict Local-First Architecture: MapsPulse operates 100% locally on your machine. All place organization, deduplication, notes management, and spreadsheet file generations happen entirely within your local browser sandbox. Zero data is transmitted to external servers, cloud databases, or third-party networks.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

MapsPulse does not access, collect, track, or transmit any user personal data:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect your name, email address, home address, or phone number.
  • No Google Account Credentials: We do not access or store your Google login, passwords, session tokens, or authentication cookies.
  • No Browsing History: We do not track your web browsing activity, tabs, or visited URLs.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not collect IP addresses, device fingerprints, MAC addresses, or hardware telemetry.
  • No Analytics or Tracking Scripts: MapsPulse contains zero third-party telemetry libraries (no Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Sentry, or tracking beacons).

3. Information Handled by MapsPulse

MapsPulse processes only the public business place details that you explicitly choose to bookmark or save:

  • Business Name: As publicly rendered on the selected place card.
  • Category / Industry: Public business category (e.g., "Dental clinic", "Electrician").
  • Contact Number: Cleaned public telephone number.
  • Website: Public direct website link.
  • Star Rating & Review Count: Public consumer rating and review volume.
  • Street Address: Public geographic location.
  • Hours & Price Level: Public operating schedule and price tier.
  • User CRM Annotations: Status tag (New, Contacted, Follow-Up, Closed) and custom notes you create.

Data Lifecycle

Lifecycle Stage Storage Mechanism Retention Period
In-Memory Curation Client-side JavaScript heap Discarded immediately upon tab navigation or closure.
Local Storage Retention Browser isolated storage (chrome.storage.local) Retained locally until you click "Clear History" or remove the extension.
User-Initiated Export Local file download (.csv, .tsv, .json) Saved directly to your local file system under your complete control.
External Transmission None 0 bytes transmitted. Zero outbound network connections.

4. Permissions Justification

  • storage: Required solely to save your bookmarked places, CRM pipeline status tags, and export history locally on your personal device without remote transmission.

5. User Data Control & Deletion

  • Clear History: Click the "Clear" button in the extension popup to instantly delete all saved place records.
  • Permanent Erasure: Uninstalling MapsPulse from Chrome (chrome://extensions) immediately and permanently purges all local storage entries.
