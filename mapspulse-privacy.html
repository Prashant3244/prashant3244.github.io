1. Overview & Single Purpose

MapsPulse is an independent productivity browser extension for Chrome designed with a single, transparent purpose: to provide a private, local business organizer and CRM companion allowing users to manage business contacts, track pipeline stages, import spreadsheets, and export local data backups.

Zero Web Page Access & Strict Local-First Architecture: MapsPulse operates as a standalone popup within its own isolated extension environment. It does not read, scrape, or extract content from any third-party website or web page. All contact management, notes, deduplication, and spreadsheet exports happen entirely within your local browser sandbox. Zero data is transmitted to external servers, cloud databases, or third-party networks.

2. Categories of Information Handled

MapsPulse handles only the user-provided contact and pipeline records you explicitly enter or import:

  • Personally Identifiable Contact Information: Business names, contact persons, telephone/mobile numbers, physical street addresses, and website links entered manually or imported via CSV.
  • CRM Workflow Annotations: Pipeline stage tags (New, Contacted, Follow-Up, Closed), priority levels, and custom follow-up notes.
  • Optional Navigation Links: Stored purely as passive reference URLs without reading or extracting the destination web page.

Categories We Do NOT Collect or Handle

  • No Authentication Credentials: We never access or store passwords, Google logins, session tokens, or authentication cookies.
  • No Financial or Payment Information: We never collect, process, or store credit card numbers, bank accounts, or financial transaction data.
  • No Browsing History or Page Monitoring: MapsPulse contains zero content scripts and cannot inspect your web navigation or search history.
  • No Third-Party Website Scraping: MapsPulse does not extract or copy content from Google Maps or any external web page.

3. Data Lifecycle & Storage Architecture

MapsPulse operates strictly on a local-first paradigm. All records remain sandboxed on your personal device:

Lifecycle Stage Implementation & Storage External Transmission
Data Storage Persisted exclusively in browser local storage (chrome.storage.local). Sync disabled. 0 bytes transmitted. Never synced to external clouds.
Data Processing In-memory client-side JavaScript handles normalization, duplicate checks, and search filtering. 0 bytes transmitted. Processed 100% locally on your CPU.
Data Export Generated client-side via native Blob API (.csv with Excel BOM, .tsv, .json). 0 bytes transmitted. Saved directly to your local file system.
Data Deletion Delete individual contact cards or purge all records via the in-app "Clear All" button. 0 bytes transmitted. Uninstalling permanently wipes local storage.
Data Sharing None. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or transfer your records to any third party or advertiser. 0 bytes transmitted. Zero third-party integrations.

4. Permissions Justification

  • storage: Required solely to save your contact records, CRM pipeline stages, notes, and application settings locally on your personal device without external transmission.

5. User Data Control & Deletion

  • Clear All Records: Click the "Clear All Saved Contacts" button in the extension popup to instantly and permanently erase all saved contact records from your device.
  • Permanent Erasure: Uninstalling MapsPulse from Google Chrome (chrome://extensions) immediately and permanently purges all sandboxed local storage entries.
  • Clear History: Click the "Clear" button in the extension popup to instantly delete all saved place records.
  • Permanent Erasure: Uninstalling MapsPulse from Chrome (chrome://extensions) immediately and permanently purges all local storage entries.
