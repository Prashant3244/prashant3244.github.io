⚡ Privacy Policy for MapsPulse

Last Updated: November 2026 • Version: 29.0.0 100% Offline-First

MapsPulse ("the Extension", "we", "our") is committed to transparent, privacy-first data handling. This Privacy Policy accurately describes how user data is handled, stored, processed, exported, and deleted by our independent Revenue Intelligence Workspace and Lightweight CRM extension.

1. Categories of Information Handled

MapsPulse is designed for independent professionals, freelancers, sales representatives, and small business owners to organize business contacts, track sales pipelines, and prioritize follow-ups. It processes the following user-provided data categories:

A. User-Provided Contact Information (Personally Identifiable Information)

When you manually enter prospects or import your authorized business spreadsheets, MapsPulse processes and stores:

  • Contact & Business Names: Names of companies, accounts, or representatives you choose to track.
  • Email Addresses & Phone Numbers: Business emails and telephone numbers entered or imported for sales communication.
  • Physical Addresses: Street locations and cities associated with your business accounts.
  • Website & Navigation URLs: Website links and optional navigation URLs provided by you.
  • Sales Opportunities & Deal Values: Deal titles, estimated values, currency, pipeline stage (New, Qualified, Proposal, Negotiation, Won, Lost), win probability percentages, and expected close dates.
  • Activity History & Follow-ups: Manually logged phone calls, meeting summaries, follow-up tasks, due dates, priority levels, and strategy notes.

B. Categories We Do NOT Collect or Handle

  • NO Authentication Credentials: We never access, capture, or store passwords, Google login credentials, session tokens, or authentication cookies.
  • NO Financial or Payment Credentials: We never collect, process, or store credit card numbers, bank accounts, or payment instruments.
  • NO Web Browsing History or Page Monitoring: MapsPulse contains zero content scripts. It does not monitor your browser tabs, web browsing activity, search queries, or visited URLs.
  • NO Third-Party Website Scraping: MapsPulse does not extract or copy content from Google Maps or any other external website.
  • NO Telemetry or Tracking Beacons: We do not include Google Analytics, Sentry, Mixpanel, or any third-party tracking scripts. Zero outbound network traffic.

2. Data Storage, Processing & Transmission Distinction

Operation Implementation & Scope External Transmission
Storage Persisted strictly on your personal device using chrome.storage.local. Never synced to cloud servers (chrome.storage.sync is completely disabled). 0 bytes transmitted
Processing In-memory client-side JavaScript handles normalization, duplicate detection, weighted revenue calculations, funnel analytics, and explainable prioritization. 0 bytes transmitted
Export User-directed generation of .csv, .tsv, and .json files via the browser's native Blob API, saved directly to your local file system or copied to your clipboard. 0 bytes transmitted
Deletion User can delete individual deals/contacts or wipe the entire database with the in-app "Reset Workspace" button. Uninstalling the extension permanently purges all sandboxed local storage. 0 bytes transmitted
Sharing We do not sell, rent, monetize, or transfer your contact records to any third party, advertiser, or data broker. 0 bytes transmitted

3. Permissions Justification

  • storage: Required solely to persist user-entered customer accounts, opportunity records, follow-up reminders, and application settings locally within the browser on the user's personal device. No network or broad access permissions are requested.
  • alarms: Required solely to schedule low-power periodic reminders and update the toolbar action badge for pending follow-up tasks without continuous background polling.

4. Chrome Web Store Limited Use Compliance

MapsPulse adheres strictly to the Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies, including the User Data Policy and Limited Use requirements:

  1. Data use is strictly restricted to providing the primary user-facing purpose: local business revenue intelligence, opportunity pipeline tracking, and follow-up discipline.
  2. Data is never transferred or sold to third parties.
  3. Data is never used for personalized advertising or credit assessment.
  4. Humans cannot read your stored data; all data remains sandboxed on your local device.
