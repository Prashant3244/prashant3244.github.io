1. Overview & Single Purpose

MapsPulse is a specialized productivity browser extension designed for Google Chrome with a single, clear purpose: to allow users to extract publicly displayed business contact information from Google Maps search results in user-navigated tabs and export them into structured CSV spreadsheets or clipboard formats.

Core Architectural Principle: MapsPulse operates on a strict local-first architecture. All DOM analysis, lead extraction, deduplication, and file generation take place entirely inside your local browser memory and sandbox. No data is transmitted to external servers or third-party cloud infrastructure.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

MapsPulse does not access, collect, track, or transmit any user personal data:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect your name, email address, home address, or phone number.
  • No Google Account Credentials: We do not inspect, access, or store your Google login, passwords, session tokens, or authentication cookies.
  • No User Browsing History: We do not track your web browsing activity, tabs, or visited URLs outside of the specific Google Maps tab you actively choose to scrape.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not collect your IP address, device fingerprints, MAC address, or hardware telemetry.
  • No Analytics or Tracking Scripts: MapsPulse contains zero third-party telemetry libraries (no Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Sentry, or trackers).

3. Information Processed by MapsPulse

While MapsPulse collects zero personal user data, it processes publicly displayed website content from Google Maps tabs when initiated by the user:

3.1 Public Business Information Processed

  • Business Name: As rendered on the public listing.
  • Category / Industry: Public business category (e.g., "Dental clinic", "Plumber").
  • Public Phone Number: Cleaned and formatted contact number.
  • Public Website Link: Direct business website URL (tracking redirects unwrapped).
  • Star Rating & Review Count: Public consumer rating and review volume.
  • Street Address: Public geographic location as listed on the card.
  • Google Maps Listing URL: Direct permanent link to the place on Google Maps.
  • Search Query String: User's current search keywords (e.g., "dentists in Pune") used to label export files.

3.2 Processing Stages

Lifecycle Stage Storage Mechanism Retention Period
In-Memory Processing Client-side JavaScript heap (Map and WeakSet) Discarded immediately upon tab navigation or closure.
Local Storage Retention Chrome isolated storage (chrome.storage.local) Retains up to 50 recent export batch summaries (query, count, timestamp, and 5-item preview). Deleted when history is cleared or extension is removed.
User-Initiated Export Local file download (.csv) or system clipboard (TSV) Stored directly on your device under your complete control.
External Transmission None 0 bytes transmitted. Zero outbound network calls.

4. Third-Party Disclosures & Remote Code

  • Zero Remote Calls: Audited with exactly 0 outbound HTTP/HTTPS requests (fetch: 0, XMLHttpRequest: 0, WebSocket: 0, sendBeacon: 0).
  • No Third-Party Data Sharing: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or transfer any data to third parties, advertising networks, or data brokers.
  • No Remote Code: Fully compliant with Manifest V3. All scripts are packaged locally within the extension bundle; no external JavaScript is ever evaluated at runtime.

5. User Data Control & Deletion

You have full autonomy over all local data managed by MapsPulse:

  • Clear History: Click the "Clear History" button in the extension popup to instantly delete all saved export batch records and scraped counters from your device.
  • Permanent Erasure: Removing or uninstalling MapsPulse from Google Chrome (chrome://extensions) immediately and permanently purges all local storage entries.

6. Chrome Web Store User Data & Limited Use Compliance

MapsPulse complies strictly with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements (subject to verification):

  1. MapsPulse processes Google Maps website content exclusively to provide and improve its user-facing single purpose of exporting public business leads.
  2. We do not transfer user or website data to third parties.
  3. We do not use or transfer user or website data for personalized, retargeted, or interest-based advertising.
  4. We do not permit humans to read processed data.
