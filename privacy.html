Privacy Policy for ArbitragePulse Pro

Last updated: October 2026

ArbitragePulse Pro ("we", "our", or "the extension") values and respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy details our data practices and strictly adheres to Google Chrome Web Store and Microsoft Edge Add-ons developer policies.

1. Single Purpose & Data Minimization

ArbitragePulse Pro exists solely to provide real-time e-commerce profit margin and fee calculations for online sellers and resellers browsing supported marketplace product pages (Amazon, Walmart, and eBay). We strictly practice data minimization: we do not collect, store, sell, or monetize any user personal data.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

We do NOT collect, transmit, track, or share:

  • Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email, home address, or phone number.
  • Browsing history or URLs outside of the active supported marketplace listings.
  • Marketplace account credentials, seller central passwords, or authentication cookies.
  • Search queries, shopping cart contents, or payment card details.

3. Local Client-Side Execution & Storage

All calculations (referral fees, FBA fulfillment rates, sales tax presets, ROI, and break-even points) are executed 100% locally on your device within your browser. When you save calculation presets or bookmark deal history, data is saved strictly using your browser's local sandbox (chrome.storage.local). This data never leaves your device and is never sent to our servers.

4. Justification of Browser Permissions

  • storage: Used strictly to save your custom calculation preferences (e.g., prep fees, inbound shipping rates, tax defaults) and saved deals locally on your device.
  • activeTab: Used exclusively to read public product attributes (title, price, category, dimensions, weight) on the current marketplace tab to calculate margins.
  • sidePanel: Used to display a persistent companion view alongside product pages so you can review saved deals and fee audit breakdowns without obstructing the product listing.
  • Host Permissions & Content Scripts: Used exclusively on supported e-commerce domains (Amazon, Walmart, eBay) to inject the calculation HUD, and on payment gateway endpoints (Paddle / ExtensionPay) for optional Pro license activation.

5. Payments & Pro Upgrades

Optional Pro subscriptions are processed securely by our Merchant of Record, Paddle.com / ExtensionPay. Payment details are entered directly on secure checkout pages hosted by the payment processor. We never handle, store, or have access to your payment card details.
